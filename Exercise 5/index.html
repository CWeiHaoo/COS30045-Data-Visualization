Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the Energy Star Rating system in Australia?

The Energy Rating Label helps Australian consumers compare the energy efficiency and running costs of appliances before purchasing. More stars mean higher energy efficiency.

Which household appliances consume the most electricity?

Heating and cooling systems typically account for around 40% of home energy usage, followed by water heating (25%) and refrigeration/televisions (around 15%).
