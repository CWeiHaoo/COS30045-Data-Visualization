Television Energy Efficiency

Televisions are among the most common entertainment appliances in Australian households. Screen size, display technology, and standby settings significantly affect total household electricity consumption.

Display Technologies & Power Usage

  • OLED: Delivers deep contrast by turning off individual pixels, making energy consumption depend heavily on the brightness of displayed scenes.
  • LED / LCD: Utilizes backlighting, offering predictable energy efficiency across varying brightness levels.
  • Screen Size: Larger display panels generally require more energy to illuminate.

Tips to Reduce Television Energy Costs

  • Enable automatic brightness adjustment (ambient light sensor).
  • Activate sleep timers or auto-power-off modes when idle.
  • Turn off standby mode at the wall socket if not using smart home integrations.
